Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how Aedion ("Aedion", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information, including mobile phone numbers used for text messaging.

Information we collect

We collect contact and program information needed to deliver communications, including:

  • Name, mailing address, phone number, and email address
  • Information provided by the organization sponsoring a communication program, such as membership or account identifiers
  • Records of communications, including call outcomes, message delivery status, responses given, and recordings where a call is recorded and disclosed
  • Consent records, including how and when consent to be contacted was obtained

How we use information

We use personal information to deliver the communications an individual has consented to receive, to honor opt-out and preference requests, to report outcomes to the organization sponsoring the program, to improve message quality and delivery, and to meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes. Mobile information obtained as part of the SMS consent process is not shared with third parties for marketing. This applies to all mobile opt-in data.

When we share information

We share personal information only as follows:

  • With the organization sponsoring a program, which is the party that holds the relationship with the individual
  • With service providers that carry messages and calls on our behalf, such as telecommunications carriers and messaging platforms, solely to deliver the communication
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of individuals or of Aedion

Service providers are bound by contract to protect the information and may not use it for their own purposes.

Text messaging

Individuals receive text messages only after providing consent. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of receiving any other service.

  • Message frequency varies by program and by the individual's circumstances. Most programs send between one and five messages per month.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. A single confirmation message is sent, after which no further messages are delivered for that program.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or email info@aedion.io.

Telephone calls

Calls are placed within permitted calling hours in the recipient's local time zone and are subject to attempt limits. Where a call is recorded, the recipient is informed before recording begins. Sensitive information is not left in a voicemail or disclosed to anyone other than the intended recipient.

Health information

Where Aedion handles protected health information on behalf of a covered entity, it does so as a business associate under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), subject to a written business associate agreement, and uses and discloses that information only as that agreement and applicable law permit.

Data retention and security

Information is retained for as long as needed to deliver the program, meet the sponsoring organization's retention requirements, and satisfy legal obligations, after which it is deleted or de-identified. We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, including encryption in transit, access controls, and audit logging.

Your choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, opt out of calls by following the instructions given during a call or by contacting us, and request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Requests concerning information held on behalf of a sponsoring organization may be directed to that organization or to us, and we will coordinate accordingly.

Children

Our communications are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 except where a parent or guardian is the intended recipient of a communication about a minor in their care.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
